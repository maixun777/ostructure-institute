Core Research Members

The Ω-Structure Institute is operated by a compact independent research team focused on structural science, computational cosmology, systems logic, and complex dynamics. All research is published collectively under the Institute identity.

Ω-01 – Structure Science
Generative rules, structural invariants, and meta-frameworks across physical, biological, and informational systems.

Ω-02 – CSOC / Event Physics
Code-first cosmology, event-generation logic, and signal-based causality.

Ω-03 – Systems Architecture
Multi-layer organizational logic, structural transitions, and system-wide behavior.

Ω-04 – Complex Dynamics
Emergent patterns, stability cycles, and long-horizon structural transitions.

Ω-05 – Modeling & Simulation
Abstract computational modeling and cross-domain structural analysis.

Team operates anonymously to preserve research independence and avoid institutional, political, or commercial constraints.
