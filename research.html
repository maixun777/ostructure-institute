Core Domains of Structural Science and Computational Cosmology

Home Research CSOC Structural Systems

1. Structure Science

Structure Science investigates generative rules, structural invariants, and meta-level patterns that govern behavior across physical, biological, informational, and multi-layer systems.

2. Computational Cosmology (CSOC)

CSOC explores a code-first cosmology framework in which generative informational structures precede events, sequences, and physical states. Research includes event-generation physics and causality structures.

3. Systems Architecture

Systems Architecture focuses on multi-level organization, structural logic, nested frameworks, and cross-domain system behavior. This domain studies long-term system configurations and structural transitions.

4. Complex Dynamics

Complex Dynamics examines emergent patterns, long-horizon transitions, stability cycles, feedback loops, and attractor behaviors in complex systems.
