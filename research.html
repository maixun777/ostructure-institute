Research Themes

Core Scientific Directions of the Ω-Structure Institute

1. Structure Science

The study of generative rules, meta-structures, structural invariants, and hidden order across physical, biological, and system-level environments. Research focuses on identifying universal patterns that govern the organization of complex systems.

2. Computational Cosmology (CSOC)

A code-first cosmology exploring event-generation physics, signal-based causality, and computational structures shaping spacetime behavior. CSOC aims to unify cosmological phenomena with computational principles.

3. Systems Architecture

Multi-layer structural design of organizations, networks, protocols, and civilization-level systems. Emphasis is placed on long-horizon dynamics, scalable architectures, and deep structural logic.

4. Complex Dynamics

Exploration of long-term transitions, stability cycles, emergent feedback networks, and large-scale behavior loops within complex adaptive systems.

5. Meta-Structural Analysis

Investigating cross-domain structural laws that appear consistently from micro-scale to cosmic-scale systems. This includes symmetry patterns, phase transitions, invariants, and deep generative principles.

6. Event Generation & Causal Geometry

Structural investigation into how “events” originate, propagate, and interact under generative rules. Focus on signal geometry, causal pathways, and high-dimensional event networks.
