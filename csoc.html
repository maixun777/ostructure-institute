1. Code-First Cosmology

CSOC investigates a generative cosmology model in which informational structures, abstract rules, and code-level operators precede physical events and observable states.

2. Event-Generation Physics

Rather than treating events as outcomes of prior physical states, CSOC explores the possibility that events arise from deeper generative logic layers that assign structure, timing, and interactions before physical manifestation occurs.

3. Signal-Based Causality

CSOC examines how signals, constraints, and rule-based operators shape causal relationships. This includes signal propagation models, causal filters, and structural dependencies in complex systems.

4. Multi-Scale Structure

Research explores how micro-rules generate macro-behavior, and how structural hierarchies govern transitions across cosmological, biological, and informational domains.
