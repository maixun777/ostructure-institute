About the Institute

The Ω-Structure Institute is a non-commercial independent research initiative focused on uncovering deep generative structures underlying structure science, computational cosmology (CSOC), systems architecture, and long-horizon complex dynamics. Our work explores structural rules, meta-frameworks, and generative logic shaping multi-scale behavior — from micro-structures to cosmic systems.

Core Research Group

The Institute is operated by a compact micro-research team working across structure science, event physics, system logic, and complex dynamics. All results are published collectively under the Institute identity.
