Structural Systems

Multi-Layer Frameworks, Generative Architecture, and System-Level Logic

1. Multi-Layer System Architecture

Structural Systems research explores how complex systems organize across multiple layers — from foundational generative rules to high-level behavioral patterns. This includes nested frameworks, structural hierarchies, and cross-scale coordination.

2. Generative Framework Design

This domain studies how system behavior can emerge from compact sets of generative rules. Research focuses on transformation operators, constraint layers, and invariant structures that govern long-horizon behavior.

3. System Dynamics and Transitions

Structural Systems examines how systems transition between regimes, reorganize internal architectures, and stabilize or destabilize over time. Topics include feedback loops, phase transitions, and horizon-scale structural change.

4. Cross-Domain Structural Logic

This research area focuses on universal structural principles that appear across physics, computation, biology, social systems, and cosmology. Our work seeks to identify deep generative structures shared across domains.
